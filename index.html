HOW TO PLAY CAMZO

🦎

Role 1: The Hider (Camouflage Phase)

You control a stickman placed on the background. Blend your character into your surroundings before the timer expires!

  • Right Click: Eyedropper — samples the exact pixel color from the background.
  • Left Click & Drag: Paintbrush — paints smoothly over your stickman with the sampled color.
  • Shift + Scroll Wheel: Zoom in & out with cursor as focal center (up to 6x detail).
  • Spacebar + Drag: Pan across canvas while zoomed in.
  • Ctrl + Scroll: Adjusts paint opacity (5% to 100%).
  • Mouse Scroll: Adjusts brush size from fine detail (1px) to wide coverage (64px).
🔦

Role 2: The Hunter (Hunting Phase)

The screen goes dark. A circular flashlight follows your mouse cursor.

  • Search: Pan your flashlight circle across the map to spot hidden stickmen silhouettes or paint seams.
  • Left Click: Investigate a spot!
  • Hit: Hider is found! Revealed with neon aura.
  • Miss: Every miss deducts 1 Life from all active Hiders (10 lives total). When lives reach 0, Hiders are eliminated!
